🏢 About PAPIT Photobooth Studio

PAPIT Photobooth Studio (Operated by Purab Sohil Tailor) is an innovative digital imaging and instant kiosk platform. We operate high-speed automated photo kiosks for private events, university festivals, commercial pop-ups, and corporate brand activations.

Equipped with studio lighting, professional cameras, and high-speed dye-sublimation/inkjet lab printers, our interactive kiosks allow guests to capture memorable multi-frame photos, customize aesthetic filters and text, and receive physical print strips within seconds.

🔄 Cancellation & Refund Policy

At PAPIT Photobooth Studio, we strive for 100% satisfaction with every physical print and digital session.

  • On-Site Physical Prints: Because prints are customized and generated instantly at the event kiosk, completed physical prints are generally non-refundable once printing has commenced.
  • Technical Glitches / Hardware Failure: If a payment is successfully deducted but the kiosk fails to print your photo strips due to a paper jam, printer hardware error, or system outage, our operator will either reprint your order immediately or initiate a 100% refund.
  • Refund Turnaround Time: If an automated or online payment error occurs, the refund is initiated within 24 to 48 hours. The refunded amount will be credited back to the customer's original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Card) within 5 to 7 business days as per banking and Razorpay payment gateway timelines.
  • Event Booking Cancellations: Private event kiosk bookings cancelled more than 7 days prior to the event date are eligible for a 75% refund of the advance deposit. Cancellations within 48 hours of the event are non-refundable.
📦 Shipping & Delivery Policy

PAPIT Photobooth Studio provides on-site physical print services and immediate digital delivery:

  • Physical Prints Delivery: All physical photo strips (A5 or A4 cuts) are printed and handed over to the customer instantly in-person at the photobooth counter immediately following photo capture (typically within 45–90 seconds). No postal shipment is required for on-site kiosk sessions.
  • Digital Files Delivery: Digital soft copies of the captured photos and strips are made available instantly on the kiosk screen via a dynamic QR code for immediate smartphone download.
